Frequently asked questions

International logistics and freight forwarding

Help centre

Start here.

What information is needed for a quote?

Prepare the goods description, origin and destination, package count, weight, dimensions and cargo-ready date. Note any unknown details in your request.

How do I choose a transport method?

Consider timing, route, dimensions and the nature of the goods together. If you have not selected a method, choose “Advice needed” in the quote form.

Does this website provide a confirmed price?

No. This website does not calculate a confirmed price. The form prepares your request; charges and final conditions must be confirmed separately.

Do special goods require additional information?

Yes. Mention batteries, chemicals, liquids, temperature requirements or unusual dimensions. Acceptance and transport conditions need review before booking.

Which documents should I prepare initially?

A goods description, packing list and origin/destination details are a useful starting point. Exact documentation depends on the goods and route and must be confirmed for the shipment.

Does my cargo need special packaging?

Describe fragility, weight, dimensions and storage requirements so packaging needs can be reviewed. Packaging suitability cannot be confirmed without reviewing the goods.

Does the form send my enquiry?

Not at present. Your information is prepared in your browser and can be downloaded as a text file. No submission confirmation or booking number is generated.

What do I need to follow up on a shipment?

Have your reference or booking number and shipment details ready for your logistics contact. This version of the website is not connected to live tracking.

Can I change my request details?

Yes. Select “Edit details” before downloading. Editing a local request does not amend any existing booking or agreement.
