About Barmana Farabar

Barmana Farabar is a Tehran-based international logistics and freight forwarding company. We plan and coordinate cargo movement by road, sea, air and rail, alongside customs clearance.

Our approach

Every shipment.

The aim is to turn your initial information into a clear understanding of the transport requirements, without promising cost or timing before the details are reviewed.

  1. 01

    Understand the cargo first

    From goods and packaging to cargo readiness, the details are the starting point for a considered plan.

  2. 02

    Choose the route

    Review road, air and ocean options around the shipment’s requirements and your priorities.

  3. 03

    Keep information clear

    Requirements, constraints and next steps should be clear before decisions are made.
